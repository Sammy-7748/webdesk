WebDesk

Consent-based browser screen sharing. No app installation.
Important: This demo shares a screen for viewing only. It cannot remotely move the mouse, type on the other computer, or access its files. The person sharing must choose a screen and can stop sharing at any time. Never share with someone you do not trust.

How to connect across two locations

  1. Both people open the published HTTPS website.
  2. The sharing person chooses I am sharing and selects a screen/window.
  3. They send the offer code to the viewer using a private, trusted method.
  4. The viewer chooses I am watching, pastes the offer, creates an answer, and sends it back.
  5. The sharing person pastes the answer and clicks Connect to viewer.

This prototype uses manual code exchange instead of a signalling server. Some networks may block a direct WebRTC connection; this free demo does not include a relay (TURN) server.

WebDesk is a demonstration project, not a secure enterprise remote-support product. Use only with the clear permission of the person sharing their screen. Do not paste connection codes into public chats.
